V51 — T-mini Plus SH 25 m /scan publication fix

Cause confirmed from 2026-08-27 field logs:
- 2-byte parser: only sparse/ring packets occasionally passed checksum.
- 3-byte parser: valid_packets=0 and checksum_fail rose for every packet.
- YDLIDAR dataset identifies Tmini Plus SH (25 m) as 16-bit intensity.
- Current SDK getPointBytes() therefore uses 4 bytes/sample.

Fix:
- Add intensity_bits parameter (default 16 for this robot).
- Parse SH sample as uint16 intensity + uint16 Q2 distance.
- XOR both 16-bit words in checksum exactly like vendor SDK.
- Convert raw triangle distance with raw/4000.0 metres.
- Do not apply legacy second-level angle correction to T-mini family.
- Propagate intensity_bits=16 through lidar, mapping, autonomous and runtime YAML migration.
- Keep /scan_nav canonical and /scan as compatibility alias.
